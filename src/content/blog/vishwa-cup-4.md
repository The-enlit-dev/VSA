Vishwa Sports Academy is proud to present **Vishwa Cup 4**, a state-level
karate championship for karate athletes across various categories, including
Sub Junior, Cadet, Junior and Senior. Hosted in affiliation with the
**Traditional Shotokan and Sports Karate Organisation**, this event is an
exciting opportunity to witness the dedication and skill of karateka from
across the state.

## Event details

- **Dates:** 21 and 22 December 2024
- **Venue:** Minatai Thakrey Skating Hall, Yamunanagar, Nigdi

Whether you're a participant or a spectator, come and be part of this display
of karate excellence.
