Are you looking to begin your karate journey or sharpen your martial arts
skills? Vishwa Sports Academy is excited to announce the launch of a new karate
class in **Aundh**, starting soon at **EuroKids Preschool, Aundh**. Whether
you're a beginner or have some experience, this is a great opportunity to get
started on the path to becoming a confident and skilled martial artist.

## Class details

- **Start date:** 1 February 2025
- **Time:** 6:30 pm to 7:30 pm
- **Days:** Monday, Wednesday, Friday

## What we offer

- **A professional karate club and training programme** with experienced,
  national-medallist coaches.
- **A one-week free demo class** to get a feel for the training and decide if
  it's the right fit for you.
- **Karate builds immunity and self-confidence** through agility, endurance,
  speed and focus-building drills.
- **Preparation for district, state, national and international tournaments.**
- **Personalised diet plans** to help students maintain a strong and healthy
  BMI.

Whether you're looking for self-defence, fitness or competitive training, our
programme is tailored for students of all ages (4 years and above), focusing on
overall physical and mental development.

**Venue:** EuroKids Preschool, Aundh
